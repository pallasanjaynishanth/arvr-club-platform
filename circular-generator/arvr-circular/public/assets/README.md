# Logo assets

Place the real logo files here (the app will pick them up automatically):

- pragati-logo.png — Pragati University logo
- arvr-logo.png — ARVR Club logo

Recommended: square PNGs, at least 200×200px, transparent background.

Until these files exist, the app shows a small labeled placeholder box in
their place (in the circular header) or hides the image (in the app
navigation/login screen) — it does not use a fake or generated logo, per
the project requirements.
